PAGE 1 — ORIGIN STORY

About me

Meanwhile, in Tamil Nadu…

Hi, I'm Ann. I'm in my first year of B.Tech Information Technology, and I'm the kind of person who learns best by building the thing and breaking it a few times along the way.

I grew up around a used car dealership in Valliyur, where people talk in both Tamil and English, sometimes in the same sentence. I think that's why I care so much about making things that feel friendly to everyone who opens them.

Plot twist

I also write. Poems, in English and in Tamil.

Code and words turned out to be the same hobby: say something clearly, then make it a little bit beautiful.
